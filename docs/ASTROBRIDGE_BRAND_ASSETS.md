# AstroBridge brand assets

AstroBridge and MA use separate product identities. AstroBridge pages use the gold bridge-shaped `A` inside the double hexagonal frame. MA product pages keep the MA application icon.

## Approved AstroBridge files

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `/assets/astrobridge-mark-256.png` | Primary navigation, headers, controls, and other 24–64 px placements |
| `/assets/astrobridge-company-logo.png` | Large company-profile presentation and structured-data logo |
| `/assets/astrobridge-og.png` | 1200 × 630 social sharing preview |
| `/assets/astrobridge-mark-256.png` and `/favicon.ico` | Browser tabs |
| `/apple-touch-icon.png` | iOS home screen |
| `/icons/astrobridge-192.png` and `/icons/astrobridge-512.png` | Web app manifest |

The high-resolution transparent presentation source is archived at `docs/brand/astrobridge-emblem-master.png`. Use it for large editorial artwork; use the optimized 256 px PNG for small navigation icons.

Use the transparent PNG mark without redrawing or compressing its proportions. Keep its tall silhouette and clear space; do not crop it into an Ethereum-style diamond, recolor individual parts, add text inside the frame, or bake a large shadow into the asset. The earlier simplified SVG exploration is retired and is not approved for product-page placement.

## MA boundary

Pages dedicated to the MA chat application use `/assets/app_icon_ma.png`. Do not replace that file with the AstroBridge company mark. Pages about AstroBridge infrastructure, the AI research forum, news, company information, trust, developer tools, consoles, and cross-chain technology use the AstroBridge mark.
