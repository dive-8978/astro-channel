Technology & products · 技术与产品

Technology for connected digital networks

English

AstroBridge & MA

AstroBridge develops software and infrastructure for digital networks. The focus is blockchain interoperability protocols and transaction routing, non-custodial transfers and wallet connectivity, online chat, and real-time communications. AI-assisted risk analysis and system protection are research directions.

AstroBridge is the infrastructure and research brand. MA is the user-facing application family through which selected communication, identity, wallet, and blockchain features are developed and evaluated.

中文

AstroBridge 与 MA

AstroBridge 研发面向数字网络的软件与技术基础设施，重点包括区块链跨链协议与交易路由、非托管转账与钱包连接，以及在线聊天与实时通信。AI 辅助风险分析和系统保护属于研发方向。

AstroBridge 是基础设施与研发品牌。MA 是面向用户的应用产品系列，用于开发和评估部分通信、身份、钱包与区块链功能。

Current research focus · 当前研发重点

Four connected technology areas

These are areas of active research and development. Availability varies by feature, network, build, and deployment stage.

01

Blockchain interoperability / 区块链互操作

Protocols and routing software for coordinating compatible requests across blockchain networks. / 面向不同区块链网络兼容请求的协议与路由软件。

02

User-controlled transfers / 用户自主转账

Non-custodial transaction flows and wallet connectivity designed to keep final authorization with the user. / 非托管交易流程与钱包连接，最终授权由用户掌握。

03

Online communications / 在线通信

Messaging, identity, signaling, and real-time communication technologies developed through the MA application family. / 通过 MA 应用产品系列研发聊天、身份、信令与实时通信技术。

04

AI-assisted security / AI 辅助安全

Research into risk analysis, anomaly detection, permission controls, and traceable safeguards for connected systems. / 研究面向互联系统的风险分析、异常检测、权限控制与可追踪保护机制。

Long-term direction · 长期发展方向

Future AI safety and security / 未来 AI 安全

Future AI safety and security is a long-term core direction for AstroBridge. / 未来 AI 安全是 AstroBridge 的长期核心发展方向。

Research direction

Safer agents, devices, and automated systems

Planned research includes trusted identity and least-privilege controls for AI agents, protection from malicious instructions and abnormal behavior, model and data privacy, auditability, meaningful human control, and secure authorization for intelligent devices and robotic collaboration.

研发方向

更安全的智能体、设备与自动化系统

计划研究 AI Agent 的可信身份与最小权限控制、恶意指令和异常行为防护、模型与数据隐私、安全审计、人类最终控制，以及智能设备和机器人协作场景中的安全授权。

  • Trusted identity, signatures, and permission boundaries / 可信身份、签名认证与权限边界
  • Prompt-injection and malicious-instruction defense / 提示注入与恶意指令防护
  • Explicit human approval for high-risk actions / 高风险操作的人类明确授权
  • Private, traceable, and auditable system design / 隐私保护、可追踪与可审计设计
  • Risk monitoring and incident response / 风险监测与事件响应
  • Safe robotics orchestration and emergency stops / 机器人安全编排与紧急停止机制
Status boundary / 状态边界These items describe future research and development. They are not claims that every capability is already in production, independently certified, or able to eliminate all AI risk. / 以上内容属于未来研发与发展方向，不表示所有能力已经投入生产、获得独立认证或能够消除全部 AI 风险。
