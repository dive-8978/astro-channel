Safety gates

Nothing starts on a promise alone.

1. Reserve fundedThe public reserve or vault balance matches the declared allocation.
2. Rules lockedToken, amount, cap, schedule and destination are immutable or timelocked.
3. Transaction confirmedThe daily action is independently readable on BNB Smart Chain.
4. Dashboard updatedOnly confirmed chain data changes the public totals.
