02 · Release logic

How status moves forward.

Progress follows evidence gates rather than a decorative wall, percentage or calendar promise.

Public foundation

Publish stable access points, product information and ownership facts.

公开稳定入口、产品信息与主体资料。

Implemented & tested

Record source, build and automated test evidence within a defined scope.

在明确范围内记录源码、构建与自动化测试证据。

Deployment acceptance

Verify production routes, configuration and approved low-value canaries.

验证生产接口、配置与获批的小额验证。

Published live status

Change public claims only after evidence is available and reviewable.

证据可查看并完成审查后，才更新公开状态。
