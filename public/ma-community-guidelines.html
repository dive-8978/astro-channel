MA's Google Play social edition is for users aged 18 and over. By entering MA and sharing content, you agree to these rules.

1. Respect and safety

Do not create, send, post, request, promote, or link to content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, sexual exploitation, pornography, threats, harassment, bullying, hate, violent extremism, scams, impersonation, malware, illegal goods or services, or other unlawful or harmful activity.

2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for content you send or post and must have the right to share it. Do not infringe copyright, privacy, publicity, trademark, or other rights. Do not expose private keys, recovery phrases, passwords, identity documents, or another person's private information.

3. Reporting and blocking

Use the in-app profile controls to block a user. Long-press a received message to report that message, or use the profile report control to report a user. Blocking prevents MA from accepting new direct messages and group invitations from that pseudonymous identity on the device.

4. Enforcement

AstroBridge may investigate reports and may restrict routing, contribution eligibility, or access associated with identities that violate these rules, protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with law. Severe child-safety or illegal-content reports may be preserved or disclosed when legally required.

5. Decentralized communications

MA uses local identities and peer-to-peer technologies, but decentralization does not remove user responsibility. Blocking and moderation cannot erase content already delivered to another participant's device.

6. Contact

Safety questions and reports may be sent to baow59671@gmail.com. Never include a private key, recovery phrase, or password.
