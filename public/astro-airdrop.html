02 / ON-CHAIN

Your allocation.
Your own one-year clock.

The planned lock starts when your allocation is successfully funded and locked on-chain—not when you register and not on one shared launch date. Each recipient has an individual unlock time.

  1. 01

    Register & verify

    Future MA QR entry and wallet-ownership verification. Registration alone does not deliver tokens or start the lock.

  2. 02

    Receive a funded lock

    The contract records the beneficiary, amount and start time. One allocation per address across this entire campaign; no early withdrawal.

  3. 03

    Withdraw after one year

    Withdrawal becomes available after the full lock. MA should show locked and available assets separately. The exact time convention and fee/sponsorship policy will be published before launch.

03 / READINESS

A plan first. A verified launch later.

MA has development integration work for our own network, but its production connection and this airdrop are not open. Mainnet parameters, asset approval, contract security and device acceptance must be completed first.

An address is not a person

The planned contract prevents repeat claims by the same beneficiary address. MA eligibility checks and anti-abuse review are also needed because one person can create several addresses.

ASTRO is distinct from MA

This plan concerns future mainnet ASTRO, not the existing MA token or a separate presale meme token. Asset form, custody and official contract details remain to be finalized.

Never share a secret

This planning page asks for no payment, private key, seed phrase, signature or token approval. No official claim contract or claim QR code has been published here.
