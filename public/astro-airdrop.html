ASTRO / TWO PROPOSED PROGRAMS

Two ways to qualify. Separate rewards.

MA app registration and MA meme-token holding are two distinct proposed paths to ASTRO mainnet rewards. Neither MA nor ASTRO has been issued; both programs are closed.

A / MA APP

Register for MA / Astro Chat

A staged community allocation after MA eligibility and wallet ownership are verified. The 1 billion ASTRO schedule below belongs to this program only. Each funded allocation is planned to lock for one year.

B / BNB CHAIN

Hold MemeAstro (MA) for six months

MA meme-token issuance is planned before ASTRO mainnet rewards. Continuous holding of the official MA token on BNB Chain for six full months is the proposed eligibility condition. MA has not been issued; no holding eligibility is established yet.

Read the MA holder plan ↗

One address may qualify for both programs, once per program after separate verification. The holder reward pool, ratio and post-distribution lock terms are pending and are not included in the registration budget. Two paths do not mean a guaranteed double payout.

Before the holder program opens: publish the official MA contract, minimum holding balance, holding-window rules, reward ratio, separate budget and distribution schedule. A single balance snapshot does not prove six months of continuous holding.

02 / PROGRAM A · ON-CHAIN

Your allocation.
Your own one-year clock.

The planned lock starts when your allocation is successfully funded and locked on-chain—not when you register and not on one shared launch date. Each recipient has an individual unlock time.

  1. 01

    Register & verify

    Future MA QR entry and wallet-ownership verification. Registration alone does not deliver tokens or start the lock.

  2. 02

    Receive a funded lock

    The contract records beneficiary, amount and start time. One allocation per address within registration program A; a separately verified holder reward must use its own program record. No early withdrawal.

  3. 03

    Withdraw after one year

    Withdrawal becomes available after the full lock. MA should show locked and available assets separately. The exact time convention and fee/sponsorship policy will be published before launch.

03 / READINESS

A plan first. A verified launch later.

MA has development integration work for our own network, but its production connection and this airdrop are not open. Mainnet parameters, asset approval, contract security and device acceptance must be completed first.

An address is not a person

Prevent duplicates by program and beneficiary without blocking two legitimate program rewards. One person can create several addresses, so MA eligibility and anti-abuse checks are also needed.

ASTRO is distinct from MA

ASTRO is the proposed mainnet reward asset; MemeAstro (MA) is the proposed BNB meme token. Neither has been issued. MA / Astro Chat is a separate software product. Official contracts and asset form are pending.

Never share a secret

This planning page asks for no payment, private key, seed phrase, signature or token approval. No official claim contract or claim QR code has been published here.
