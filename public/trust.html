Publication rule · 发布规则

Evidence before badges

Free programs and public scanners can improve transparency, but a badge is published only after the current result can be verified. / 免费项目和公开检测可以提升透明度，但只有当前结果可被核验后才会展示徽章。

  1. Prepare / 准备Publish the relevant policy, control, or technical change and identify its owner.
  2. Verify / 核验Run the external check or workflow, inspect the result, and preserve its date and scope.
  3. Describe accurately / 准确说明Publish the result with its limits; remove or update stale evidence when the underlying state changes.
