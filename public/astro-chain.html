04 / TRUST MODEL

Verified access. Explicit trust.

Network security is built through authenticated connections, clear permissions and accountable operations. Each capability is enabled only within its approved scope.

01

Verify each connection

Encrypted transport and network identity checks protect authenticated requests from reaching an unintended service.

02

Keep authority narrow

Requests are limited to explicitly permitted capabilities. Wallet signing stays with the user; access availability does not grant payment authority.

03

Make failure visible

Time limits, capacity controls and explicit availability states keep failures observable. Applications must show when a requested service is unavailable.

04

Validate the whole payment path

Publicly trusted certificates, approved contracts, signed asset permissions, device acceptance and security review are required for a real-value payment release.

08 / QUESTIONS WORTH ANSWERING

Understand the network before using it.

Does wider access mean that blockchain consensus is decentralized?

No. Network access, asset settlement and consensus serve different roles. Decentralization requires independent operators, governance and proven recovery.

Is every token free to transfer in MA?

No. The zero-fee design applies to eligible transfers of our approved native asset on our own chain. External chain gas, bridge fees and settlement conditions remain separate.

Can I make a real payment now?

New-chain payments are not open. The current work uses controlled previews and test assets; the MA phone payment path needs complete configuration and acceptance.

How do you evaluate reliability?

We evaluate measured availability, latency, capacity, recovery and operating dependencies. Any future service commitment must be supported by verified operational evidence.

Will Astro AI Chain be better than existing networks?

Our goal is an excellent network for practical applications. Claims about speed, cost or security must be supported by reproducible comparisons. No unmeasured superiority or absolute safety is promised.

What stays under the user's control?

Wallet signing remains user-authorized. Approved permissions, amounts, destinations and network identities must be explicit. A chat identity alone is not a verified payment address.
