01 / A USEFUL FOUNDATION

One ecosystem. Distinct network roles.

AI-native is our design direction: identity, bounded permissions, tasks and resource budgets are protocol concerns. These capabilities are being developed and validated in stages.

01

A general-purpose main network

The target main network provides deterministic EVM execution, accounts, smart contracts and AI protocol state for individuals, institutions and developers. Its independent validator rollout is not yet accepted.

02

Application sidechains

MA’s internal sidechain is an application subnetwork for its own transfer use cases. Each sidechain needs a distinct identity, resource policy and security model. Main-to-sidechain messaging and settlement require separate validation.

03

An AI service ecosystem

Build toward authorized AI tasks, capability discovery, measured resources and verifiable result records. Adoption and service quality will be evaluated through controlled developer pilots.

AI / PROTOCOL DIRECTION

AI needs a protocol it can safely use.

Identity, permissions, tasks and resources belong in the network design. Inference runs off-chain; consensus validates deterministic rules and recorded state. These are planned capabilities, not a claim of completed AI execution.

01

Identity and authorization

Versioned identities, scoped capabilities, expiry and revocation let services act within explicit user permission. Identity registration alone does not grant payment authority.

02

Tasks and verifiable records

Record requests, claims, result commitments and acceptance. A signature or hash can support integrity and attribution; it does not by itself prove correct inference or a truthful answer.

03

Resources and service quality

Develop capability directories, bounded budgets and measurable service quality. Intelligent scheduling must stay within policy and retain a tested fallback.

04 / TRUST MODEL

Verified access. Explicit trust.

Network security is built through authenticated connections, clear permissions and accountable operations. Each capability is enabled only within its approved scope.

01

Verify each connection

Encrypted transport and network identity checks protect authenticated requests from reaching an unintended service.

02

Keep authority narrow

Requests are limited to explicitly permitted capabilities. Wallet signing stays with the user; access availability does not grant payment authority.

03

Make failure visible

Time limits, capacity controls and explicit availability states keep failures observable. Applications must show when a requested service is unavailable.

04

Validate the whole payment path

Publicly trusted certificates, approved contracts, signed asset permissions, device acceptance and security review are required for a real-value payment release.

08 / QUESTIONS WORTH ANSWERING

Understand the network before using it.

Does wider access mean that blockchain consensus is decentralized?

No. Network access, asset settlement and consensus serve different roles. Decentralization requires independent operators, governance and proven recovery.

Is every token free to transfer in MA?

No. The zero-fee design applies to eligible transfers of our approved native asset on our own chain. External chain gas, bridge fees and settlement conditions remain separate.

Can I make a real payment now?

New-chain payments are not open. The current work uses controlled previews and test assets; the MA phone payment path needs complete configuration and acceptance.

How do you evaluate reliability?

We evaluate measured availability, latency, capacity, recovery and operating dependencies. Any future service commitment must be supported by verified operational evidence.

Will Astro AI Chain be better than existing networks?

Our goal is an excellent network for practical applications. Claims about speed, cost or security must be supported by reproducible comparisons. No unmeasured superiority or absolute safety is promised.

What stays under the user's control?

Wallet signing remains user-authorized. Approved permissions, amounts, destinations and network identities must be explicit. A chat identity alone is not a verified payment address.

What does AI-native mean here?

It is our design direction: identity, bounded authorization, tasks, capability discovery and resource budgets become protocol concerns. Inference stays off-chain, and AI cannot override signatures, balances or deterministic validation. The full capability set is not yet delivered.

Is Astro AI Chain only the MA transfer network?

No. The target is a general-purpose AI-native main network with application sidechains. MA’s internal sidechain is one part. Each chain has its own trust model, and their connection needs separate validation.
