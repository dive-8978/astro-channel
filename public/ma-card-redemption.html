Security by design

The browser never holds the keys.

Wallet private keys will never be stored in the page. Claims require server-issued inventory, single-use challenges, replay protection and auditable on-chain settlement.

Current safety boundary

Today this page is read-only. It cannot inspect local app cards, sign for a user, connect a wallet or transfer MA.
