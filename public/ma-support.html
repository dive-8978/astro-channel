02 / CHECK BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Recognize the right account.

When account details are published, compare the complete chat ID with this official directory before adding a contact. An account name, avatar or forwarded QR code is not enough.

A MA chat identity is separate from a funds-wallet address. This page will identify the chat account specifically; a payment address is not a customer-support login.

KEEP YOUR SECRETS PRIVATE

Help should never require your keys.

Never share your recovery phrase, private keys, passwords or verification codes with anyone claiming to be support.

For an issue report, prepare the app version, operating system, steps to reproduce and expected versus actual result. Remove sensitive information from screenshots, and share reports only with a verified contact.
