01 / THE MA DIFFERENCE

An identity beyond your phone number.

MA uses a cryptographic address for chat identity. No phone number is required to create that identity. The chat identity and the funds wallet are separate.

01

Address-based identity

Chat authentication uses a signing key, rather than an SMS verification code. Protecting that key and your device remains essential.

02

Conversations with wallet tools

Access selected EVM transfers alongside chat. Always confirm the actual receiving wallet and network; a chat ID is not a verified payment address.

03

Local-first experiences

Local records, reading and playful garden experiences share one app. Network services and public blockchain activity have their own privacy boundaries.

Security is a responsibility, not a slogan.

MA is not a hardware cold wallet. Device loss, malware, phishing and key exposure remain risks; public transfers can be traceable. Current local key storage needs further hardening. We make no global-first, absolute-security or absolute-privacy claim.
