01 / THE MA DIFFERENCE

An identity beyond your phone number.

MA uses a cryptographic address for chat identity. No phone number is required to create that identity. The chat identity and the funds wallet are separate.

01

Address-based identity

Create a chat identity without a phone number. Signing-key authentication gives MA an address-based account model independent of SMS.

02

Conversations with wallet tools

Move from a conversation to wallet tools, receive with a QR code and keep transfer records locally. Confirm the receiving wallet and network before authorizing a payment.

03

Local-first experiences

Keep conversations and playful experiences close at hand, with local records and seven interface languages.

Check before you send.

Confirm the recipient wallet, network and amount. MA and AstroBridge cannot cancel a confirmed on-chain transfer. Funds sent to the wrong address may be unrecoverable.

ASTROBRIDGE / THE TECHNOLOGY

One intent. A coordinated path.

A non-custodial intent API architecture for wallets, exchanges, Web3 applications and AI agents. Applications describe the requested action; the network design coordinates authorization checks, risk assessment, routing and status.

  1. 01User-signed intent
  2. 02Identity & risk checks
  3. 03Route & coordinate
  4. 04Track execution
01

Signed-intent interface

A shared request structure carries the action, address, network, timestamp and signature. Intent identifiers make the request traceable through its lifecycle.

02

Authorization stays with the user

AstroBridge is designed to verify and route requests without taking custody of assets or keeping user wallet private keys. Required on-chain actions remain wallet-authorized.

03

Connected service layers

AstroChannel provides the integration interface; P2P channels carry messages and state; market aggregation and caching reduce repeated requests from mobile clients.

04

Built for progressive expansion

The whitepaper describes health-aware node scheduling, offline synchronization, AI-assisted risk and routing, low-frequency MiniBTC checkpoints and NFT authorization channels.

Architecture overview based on the July 2026 AstroBridge v7 and MA V2 whitepapers. Current validation focuses on BSC/EVM workflows. Broader chain support and production settlement are staged roadmap items, not blanket live availability.

ASTRO AI CHAIN / THE DESIGN VISION

Intelligence that collaborates.
Trust that can be checked.

A network vision for people, applications and AI agents: authorized work, verifiable identity and a shared record of outcomes.

  1. 01

    Identity & ledger

    The design records identity, permissions and task status. Deterministic rules—not model opinions—govern ledger changes.

  2. 02

    AI & coordination

    Agents plan, use approved tools and check outcomes within a defined budget, scope and deadline.

  3. 03

    Authorized resources

    A proposed resource layer connects permitted compute, storage and services. Private data does not become public by joining the network.

Distinct roles, connected purpose: MA is an application entry point; AstroBridge is an independent integration partner; Astro AI Chain is the network under development.

Versioned design summary · V1.2, 12 October 2026. The paper documents development-network work, not a production-mainnet or global-capacity certification. Later releases need separate verification.
