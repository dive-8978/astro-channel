01 / KEEP GOOD MACHINES WORKING

More useful years.
Fewer dead ends.

A missing component should never be the end of a robot’s story. AstroPart is building a shared starting point for owners, manufacturers and repair teams to find what fits and where to get it.

From the robot in your home to the machines in a factory, our ambition is the same. Make maintenance easier to understand, easier to access and easier to trust.

02 / A SHORTER PATH TO REPAIR

Scan. Find. Keep going.

One identity connects the machine, its components and the people who can help.

  1. 01
    ⌗

    Know your robot

    A QR or supported NFC label opens the robot or part record. Start with the model, manufacturer and the information made available for that machine.

  2. 02
    ⎇

    Find what fits

    Check compatible parts and regional supply information. See who to contact, the service area and when availability was last confirmed.

  3. 03
    ↻

    Keep the story

    Owners can authorize repair records so the next service starts with a better understanding of the robot’s history.

03 / PART OF ASTRO AI CHAIN

Every robot has a story.
Give it a lasting place.

Our vision is free on-chain registration for robots and their components, bringing identity and a verifiable repair history into the Astro AI Chain ecosystem.

◎

◇

Free component registration

↗

A connected repair record

Help authorized owners and repair teams verify the history behind a machine.

ON-CHAIN ROLLOUT / IN DEVELOPMENT

Free on-chain registration is the planned service. The current pilot provides lookup and application records; chain registration will open after integration and acceptance. Parts, repairs and delivery are priced by their providers.
