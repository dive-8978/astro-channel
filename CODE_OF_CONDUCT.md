# Community standards

AstroBridge welcomes technically grounded, good-faith participation.

Be respectful, focus criticism on work and evidence, protect personal and
security-sensitive information, and do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or
mislead others. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that
violates these standards.

Report security matters privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report
other conduct concerns to the maintainers through the repository's private
contact channels.
